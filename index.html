How Triply Works

From initial inspiration to final bill settlement, here is how Triply powers your journeys.

1

Build Your Itinerary

Chat with Triply's AI travel agent or choose a curated getaway template. Get a day-by-day plan with top sights, hotels, and dining tailored to your style and budget.

2

Share 1-Tap Invite Link

Tap "Copy Link" to share a direct trip invite via WhatsApp, Messages, or email. When friends tap the link on their iPhone, they instantly join your trip.

3

Track & Settle Bills

Log group expenses as you travel. Triply calculates minimal debt transactions so settling up at the end of the trip takes just seconds without messy spreadsheets.

Everything for Smarter Trips

AI Trip Planner

Chat with Triply's AI to generate tailored day-by-day itineraries packed with top attractions, hidden spots, and local dining that match your travel style and budget.

Trip Collaboration

Invite friends with a direct link. Real-time sync keeps the entire group aligned on itinerary stops, schedules, and places to see without messy group chats.

Privacy & Cloud Sync

Sign in securely with Apple. Your trip details, expenses, and itinerary notes are encrypted and safely backed up to the cloud across your devices.
